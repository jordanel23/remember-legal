Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

The short version

Remember is a private journal. Everything you write, your Memories, Reflections, Connections, Threads, Focuses, Archive, and Recently Deleted, is stored only on your device. There is no account, no login, and no server copy of your journal. When Remember checks whether a new entry connects to something you wrote before, a small, relevant slice of your own past entries (never your whole journal) is sent once to Anthropic for that single comparison, and is not used to train any model.

What stays on your device

Every Memory, Reflection, Connection, Thread, and Focus you create lives in a local database on your phone. Archiving, deleting, or restoring an entry only ever changes that local database. If you delete the app or lose your device without a device backup, that content is gone; Remember has no copy to restore it from.

What Remember sends to Anthropic (Claude), and why

To look for a genuine connection between a new entry and your past ones, Remember sends that new entry, a short, on-device-selected shortlist of your own past entries, and the names of any Focuses you have added, to Claude, Anthropic's AI model, for that one comparison. That is never your entire journal. This request passes through a backend function that exists only to relay it; Remember does not store a copy of your journal text there. Anthropic processes this text to return its judgment and does not use API requests like this to train its models.

App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, your PIN is stored securely on your device (in the device's secure hardware-backed storage) and is never transmitted anywhere, including to Remember's own backend.

Subscriptions

Remember+ purchases are handled by Apple or Google through their standard in-app purchase systems, using RevenueCat to verify your subscription status. Remember never sees or stores your payment card details. RevenueCat and the App Store/Play Store receive the standard purchase and transaction information needed to process and verify a subscription.

Feedback and support requests

If you submit a bug report, problem report, or feature request from Help & Support, Remember sends that message, an optional email address if you choose to provide one, and basic technical context (app version, platform, OS version, device model) to Remember's backend so it can be reviewed and, if you left an email, responded to. This never includes your journal content.

What Remember does not do

Remember does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers, does not sell or share your data with advertisers, and does not read or access your journal content on its own servers. Remember is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can export your memories as plain text at any time from Settings, and you can permanently erase everything Remember has stored on your device from Settings → Delete All Data. Because your journal is never stored on a server, there is nothing further for Remember to delete on its end once you do.

Changes to this policy

Remember may update this policy as the app changes. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. This policy was last updated September 17, 2026.
